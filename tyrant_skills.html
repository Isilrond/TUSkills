Overload
Support

Give Overload to an ally Assault, causing its skills to become unavoidable and irreducible.

  • Lasts until end of player's turn.
  • Unavoidable means skills cannot be avoided by Evade or Inhibit.
  • Irreducible means damage from skills cannot be reduced by Protect, Barrier, or Stasis.
  • Application of Overload can be blocked by Inhibit if the caster is not Overloaded.
  • Only targets Assaults with valid skills - Enfeeble, Jam, Siege, Mortar, Strike or Weaken.
  • If any allied units are Inhibited, then Heal, Protect, Enrage, and Rally are also considered valid skills.
  • Only targets active Assaults that have not acted yet this turn and do not already have Overload.
